Industrial IoT (IIoT) development services

We build industrial IoT systems that connect the equipment you already run to one dashboard: edge hardware, firmware, protocols, a cloud platform and the apps on top. We have done it for energy monitoring, steel furnaces, water treatment and construction sites.

Book an intro call See industrial projects

What we build

  • Monitoring and dashboards

    Live and historical views of energy, temperature, flow, level, assets and people, with alerts when a value crosses a limit.

  • Edge gateways and firmware

    Devices that read your meters and sensors, send the data on, and update over the air.

  • Protocol connectivity

    Modbus over RS485 and TCP, LoRaWAN, MQTT, HTTPS and Bluetooth Low Energy, plus UART, RS-232, SPI and I2C on the device itself.

  • Cloud platform

    Time-series storage, alerts, device provisioning, and role-based access so each client or site sees only its own units.

  • Custom hardware and sensors

    Circuit design and PCB layout, analog inputs (4 to 20 mA and 0 to 5 V), thermocouple readers, and displays for local use.

  • Support and updates

    Firmware updates over the air, monitoring of the system after launch, and fixes when conditions on site change.

Works with the equipment you already have

Most plants cannot replace machines just to monitor them. We read from what is already there: energy meters over Modbus, thermocouples, level and flow sensors, 4 to 20 mA and 0 to 5 V signals, and Bluetooth tags on people and assets.

If a machine gives no signal at all, we say so. Sometimes a clamp-on sensor is the answer. Sometimes the honest advice is that monitoring this machine will not pay off.

What we ask before we quote

  • What equipment, and what signals does it give (Modbus registers, analog, none)?
  • How many points, on how many sites, and how often do you need a reading?
  • Who needs to see the data, and with what permissions?
  • Which systems should the data flow into later (spreadsheet, SCADA, ERP or MES)?
  • What happens on site when the network is down?
  • Is this a pilot on one line or a rollout?

Questions we get

Can you connect machines that are not “smart”?
Yes, when there is a signal to read: a meter with Modbus, an analog output, a thermocouple, or a place to fit a sensor. If there is no signal and no place for a sensor, we tell you before you spend.
Which protocols do you support?
Modbus, LoRaWAN, MQTT, HTTPS and Bluetooth Low Energy on the field side. Others are scoped per project.
How do you keep one client’s data away from another’s?
Our platforms use role-based access with separate accounts for administrators, OEMs and clients, so each user sees only their own sites and devices.
Can the data go into our ERP, SCADA or MES?
Integrations are scoped per project. Tell us the system and we will say what is needed.
How long does it take?
It depends on the number of sites and the hardware. The written plan includes a timeline.
How much does it cost?
We do not publish prices. We quote after the intro call, once we know the equipment, the points and the number of sites.
