IoT staff augmentation: add embedded and firmware engineers to your team

UTF Labs adds embedded and firmware engineers to your team. They work on your product, under your priorities. Hire by the hour, as monthly dedicated engineers, or as a dedicated team. An engineer can start in about a week, and your first 40 hours are a risk-free trial.

Talk about your team See how the trial works

The short answer

IoT staff augmentation means adding engineers from UTF Labs to your own team. They work on your product with your priorities and your tools. You can hire by the hour, as monthly dedicated engineers, or as a dedicated team. An engineer can start in about a week, and the first 40 hours are a risk-free trial.

When it fits, and when it does not

It fits when

  • your roadmap is slipping and hiring locally is slow;
  • you need a specific skill for a while (firmware, OTA updates, secure boot, embedded Linux, circuit boards);
  • you are moving from prototype to production and need extra hands;
  • you have technical leadership in-house and need more capacity.

It does not fit when

  • nobody on your side can set technical direction. Then you need a project built for you, not extra engineers (see IoT product development);
  • you need cloud or app developers only. We build those as projects (see IoT app development), not as placed engineers.

Not sure which one you need? Tell us. We will say which.

Who you can hire

  • Embedded firmware engineers

    STM32, ESP32, nRF and Bluetooth Low Energy, Silicon Labs, Zephyr and other RTOS.

  • OTA and secure boot

    Over-the-air update systems and secure boot for devices in the field.

  • Embedded Linux

    Drivers and board support packages (BSP).

  • PCB design

    Schematics and board layout.

Four experience levels: junior, mid, senior and specialist. We suggest a level from your brief, and you can ask for a different one. Specialists cover Zephyr, OTA and secure boot, nRF and Silicon Labs, and Linux drivers.

Three ways to engage

Hourly

You pay for the hours worked. Best when the amount of work changes from week to week.

Monthly dedicated engineers

One or more engineers dedicated to your product each month. Best for steady, long-running work.

Dedicated team

A team assigned to your product that works as an extension of yours. Best for a larger build.

The risk-free trial

Every engagement starts with a trial.

  • First 40 hours at half rate

    If you are happy, work continues at the full rate.

  • If you are not happy

    Those 40 hours are not invoiced, and there is no lock-in after the trial.

  • Then a replacement engineer

    We offer a replacement engineer for a second 40-hour trial at half rate. If that one does not work out either, those hours are not invoiced.

Working across time zones

Full working-day overlap is available with US and European teams, in any US time zone. Tell us your hours in the intro call.

What can go wrong, and what we do about it

Augmentation has real risks. We would rather you hear them from us.

  • Knowledge sits in one person’s head

    We write documentation and notes into your own repository as we go, so the work stays with you.

  • A slow start

    New engineers need time to learn your product. We ask for access, a short brief and one point of contact on day one.

  • A poor fit

    That is what the trial and the replacement engineer are for.
