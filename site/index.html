Three ways to work with us

Build a connected product

From first prototype to production: board design, firmware, cloud platform and a mobile or web app. We took TickrMeter, a desk e-ink stock ticker, from prototype through firmware, over-the-air updates and manufacturing tests.

Add industrial IoT to sites you already run

Monitor energy, temperature, assets and people without replacing your equipment. We connect existing meters and sensors over Modbus, LoRaWAN and MQTT and bring the data into one dashboard.

Quick answers

Questions we get first

What is UTF Labs?

UTF Labs is an IoT and software engineering company founded in July 2017. We build connected products and industrial IoT systems, and we add engineers to client teams. We have offices in Washington, DC, Berlin and Karachi.

Who do you work with?

Product companies and industrial operators in the US, Europe and Australia, from start-ups building a first device to plants that need monitoring on existing equipment.

Can you add engineers to our team?

Yes. You can hire by the hour, as monthly dedicated engineers or as a dedicated team. An engineer can start in about a week, and the first 40 hours are a risk-free trial.

Do you publish prices?

No. Cost depends on the scope or the engineers you need, so we quote after an intro call.
