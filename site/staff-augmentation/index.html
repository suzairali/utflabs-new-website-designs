The short answer

IoT staff augmentation means adding engineers from UTF Labs to your own team. They work on your product with your priorities and your tools. You can hire by the hour, as monthly dedicated engineers, or as a dedicated team. An engineer can start in about a week, and the first 40 hours are a risk-free trial.

Fit

When it fits, and when it does not

It fits when

  • your roadmap is slipping and hiring locally is slow;
  • you need a specific skill for a while (firmware, OTA updates, secure boot, embedded Linux, circuit boards);
  • you are moving from prototype to production and need extra hands;
  • you have technical leadership in-house and need more capacity.

It does not fit when

  • nobody on your side can set technical direction. Then you need a project built for you, not extra engineers (see IoT development);
  • you need cloud or app developers only. We build those as projects (see IoT app development), not as placed engineers.

Not sure which one you need? Tell us. We will say which.

How the trial works

The risk-free trial

Every engagement starts with a trial.

  • First 40 hours at half rate. If you are happy, work continues at the full rate.
  • If you are not happy, those 40 hours are not invoiced, and there is no lock-in after the trial.
  • Then we offer a replacement engineer for a second 40-hour trial at half rate. If that one does not work out either, those hours are not invoiced.

Honest risks

What can go wrong, and what we do about it

Augmentation has real risks. We would rather you hear them from us.

A poor fit.

FAQ

Questions we get

How fast can an engineer start?

Usually in about seven days. More engineers beyond that take two to four weeks.

What does the trial cost?

The first 40 hours are billed at half rate. If you are not happy, they are not invoiced. There is no lock-in after the trial.

What if the engineer is not a good fit?

We offer a replacement engineer for a second 40-hour trial on the same terms.

Do you publish rates?

No. We quote after an intro call.

Who directs the work?

You do. You set priorities and tasks, and the engineers work in your tools and repository. If a UTF Labs project manager coordinates day to day, they take the work from you.

Who owns the code?

You do. The code is your property.

Do you offer cloud or app developers?

Not as placed engineers. We build cloud platforms and apps as projects.
